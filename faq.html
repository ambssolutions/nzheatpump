Questions and answers

We work it out on site. Room size matters, but so do ceiling height, insulation, glazing and how cold the room gets on a frosty morning. Online calculators give a starting point; a visit gives you the right answer.

Not always. A heat pump normally gets its own circuit. Some older switchboards have no room for one or need upgrading first. We check this at the site visit and put it on the quote.

A straightforward high-wall install is usually done within a day. Long pipe runs, multi-split and ducted systems, or switchboard work take longer. Your quote will say.

Somewhere with good airflow, on a solid base or wall bracket, and ideally away from bedroom windows, yours or the neighbours'. We show you the options before we start.

Most manufacturers recommend a professional service once a year. Between services, clean the indoor filters every few weeks when the unit is working hard. Your manual shows how.

A little frost on a cold morning is normal. The unit melts it off in a short defrost cycle, and you may see steam. Ice that keeps building and does not clear needs a look.
