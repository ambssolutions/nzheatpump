For landlords

The Healthy Homes heating standard

18°C

Rental homes must have fixed heating that can warm the main living room to at least 18°C. A correctly sized heat pump is the usual way to meet it.

  • Sized to the living room, not a rule of thumb
  • Install and electrical done together, with certificates
  • Servicing to keep it running between tenancies
