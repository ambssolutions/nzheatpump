Which type suits your home

The right type depends on the room, the layout of the house and where the units can go. We talk it through at the site visit.

TypeSuitsGood to know
High-wallLiving areas and bedroomsThe most common choice. The indoor unit sits high on the wall, the outdoor unit outside on a base or bracket.
Floor consoleRooms with little high wall space, or under a windowSits low like a heater, so warm air comes out near the floor where you feel it.
Multi-splitTwo or more rooms sharing one outdoor unitFewer units on the outside of the house. Each room keeps its own controls.
DuctedHeating and cooling the whole homeHidden in the ceiling or under the floor, with a small outlet in each room. Needs enough ceiling or underfloor space.

Replacing an old heat pump

Heat pumps do wear out. If yours is loud, slow to warm the room on a frosty morning, or keeps needing repairs, a new unit is often the better spend.

  • We remove the old unit and recover its refrigerant properly
  • Existing pipe runs and brackets are reused only where they are sound
  • We check the circuit is right for the new unit

Servicing and repairs

A clean heat pump heats better, costs less to run and lasts longer. Most manufacturers recommend a professional service once a year.

What a service covers

  • Filters, indoor coil and fan cleaned
  • Condensate drain checked and cleared
  • Outdoor unit cleaned and cleared of leaves and debris
  • Electrical connections checked
  • Heating and cooling tested before we leave

Signs it needs a look

Usually blocked filters or a dirty indoor coil. Clean the filters first. If it is still weak, it is time for a service.

Mould and dust build up on the indoor coil and fan, especially in damp Waikato winters. A deep clean deals with it.

Most often a blocked condensate drain. Turn the unit off and call us before the water marks the wall.

Some frost on a cold morning is normal; the unit melts it off in a short defrost cycle. Ice that builds up and does not clear needs a look.

Write down the code or take a photo of the lights and send it with your enquiry. It helps us bring the right parts.
