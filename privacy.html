Your privacy.

How we collect, use and look after the details you send us, under the Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

When you use our enquiry form or contact us, we collect what you choose to give us: usually your name, phone number, email, town and details of the job.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, quote for and carry out the work, and keep records we are required to keep, such as electrical certificates. We do not sell or share your details for marketing.

Where it is stored

Enquiries arrive by email through our form provider. We keep job records for as long as we need them for the work, warranties and our legal obligations.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies. If we add visitor statistics in future, we will say so here.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you. Contact us using the details on our contact page. If you are not happy with how we handle your request, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.
