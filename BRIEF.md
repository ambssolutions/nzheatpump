# Brief: NZ Heat Pump Services

## What we were told
- An electrical and heat pump business working in the Waikato region, New Zealand.
- Name given as "NZ heat Pumps services". The site uses **NZ Heat Pump Services** until the client confirms the exact spelling.
- The client was very impressed with deltaair.co.nz (an AMBS heat pump site). This site has to impress just as much, while looking and moving completely differently.

## What we found
- No existing website or online listing under this name (checked 20 October 2026: no site at nzheatpumpservices.co.nz, nzheatpumps.co.nz is a parked domain). So there is no copy, logo, photos, reviews or contact details to reuse yet.

## How the site handles that
- Copy describes the trade in general, honest terms: how sizing works, what an install involves, the certificates NZ law requires (Certificate of Compliance, Electrical Safety Certificate). No numbers, prices, years in business, awards, brands or reviews are claimed.
- Contact details, licence line and reviews sit empty in `assets/business.js` and stay hidden until supplied.
- Photos are illustrative Unsplash images, labelled as such. Heat pumps are shown as line drawings until we have real job photos.
- Service list (heat pump installs, replacements, servicing, repairs; switchboards, circuits, lighting, renovation wiring, fault finding, heat pump circuits) is a typical scope for this kind of business and **must be confirmed by the client**.

## Design direction: "the live wire"
- Palette: limestone paper `#f5f2eb`, sand `#ebe4d6`, frost `#e2eae7`, river-green ink `#13231f`, copper `#a3461a` / `#e08a55`. Swap the accents for the real logo colours when it arrives.
- Type: Archivo (condensed, 700) for headings, IBM Plex Sans for text.
- Surface: square corners, hairline rules, flat copper buttons with a soft light sweep, a spec table, line drawings.
- Signature: a copper wire feeds each section; frost-to-warm hero; Waikato River map with current running down it; the "Too small / Sized right" room demo.
