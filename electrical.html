The paperwork you should get

Electrical work on your home must be done by a licensed electrician. When it is finished, the law requires a record of it.

  • Certificate of Compliance (CoC): records the work that was done and that it meets the standards.
  • Electrical Safety Certificate (ESC): confirms new work is safe to connect to supply.

Keep both with your house documents. Insurers and future buyers may ask for them.
